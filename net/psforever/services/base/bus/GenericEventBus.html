GenericEventBus

class GenericEventBus extends ActorEventBus with SubchannelClassification

Maintain a list of endpoints that have subscribed to a specific perspective internally called a Classifier and externally referred to as a "channel". When an Event - externally referred colloquially as a "message" - is received, to be published, match the "channel" to a list of known Classifier endpoints. Each of these Subscribers should receive the message.
